## M — Messages (SECURITY slice)

**Owner:** pilotfish:security-executor (branch `m2-m`). Uses W0.5 `Rest` and the W0.4 observer.

| Rule | Contract (sources) |
|---|---|
| Scope | Guild channels and DMs. **Target rule:** the open target is `isDMMode ? selectedDMID : selectedChannelID`, the same rule as `ShellState.header`; nil means nothing is open. AppModel's `selectionChanged` opens **`ShellState.openTarget`** (W0.6) and reads nothing else. `send_message` posts only to the currently **open** channel id, never to a selection read at submit time. M gets its store from W0's `SessionHandle::messages()` and its REST client from `rest()`, and fills the W0 stub `messages.rs`. |
| History | One GET of the latest 50 per open (typed method on `Rest`, `u64` ids only). Discord returns them newest first; the buffer is sorted ascending by snowflake id and deduplicated by id. GET results are **merged into** events that arrived during the fetch, not replaced by them. Cap 200, dropping the oldest. Leaving the channel cancels its in-flight GET. |
